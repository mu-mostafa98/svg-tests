SVG Engine Test Suite: Linear Gradients

Test 5.1: Default Axis Orientation

Expect: Horizontal gradient transitioning smoothly from left (blue) to right (green). Omitting structural vector coordinates must resolve to default horizontal settings.

Test 5.2: Varying Angles (Diagonal & Vertical)

Expect: Top rectangle blends vertically from red down to orange. Bottom rectangle blends diagonally from top-left (purple) down to bottom-right (light blue).

Test 5.3: Multiple Color Stops

Expect: Smooth multi-band progression from left to right: red, yellow, green, then blue. Color transitions must scale gracefully at their designated offset positions.

Test 5.4: Opacity Stops

Expect: Black overlay gradient. Solid black on the far edges, fading to near transparency in the center, allowing the background red vertical bars to become highly visible.

Test 5.5: Spread Method - Pad

Expect: Dynamic color blend between 30% and 70%. Areas before 30% are clamped to solid blue. Areas after 70% are clamped to solid yellow.

Test 5.6: Spread Method - Reflect

Expect: Mirrored repeating ribbons. Transitions outwards from green to purple, back to green, repeating mirrored patterns across boundaries seamlessly.

Test 5.7: Spread Method - Repeat

Expect: Sawtooth repeating intervals. Loops sharply from green back to purple at boundaries without mirroring, forming abrupt hard structural seams.

Test 5.8: Coordinate Spaces
