SVG Engine Test Suite: Radial Gradients

Test 6.1: Default Implicit Center

Expect: Symmetric circular gradient originating precisely from the geometric center of the canvas. Omitting coordinates must resolve to 50% defaults.

Test 6.2: Asymmetric Focal Point

Expect: Pseudo-3D spherical illumination. The white hotspot core is shifted to the upper-left (25%, 25%), while the gradient rings stretch towards the centered circular border.

Test 6.3: Focal Radius (fr) Core

Expect: A solid purple core disk forms in the absolute center spanning 15% out before any color interpolation towards blue begins to execute.

Test 6.4: BBox Unit Aspect Ratio

Expect: The gradient rings stretch out into a flat horizontal ellipse. Under default `objectBoundingBox` settings, non-square targets distort the gradient aspect ratio.

Test 6.5: UserSpace Aspect Preservation

Expect: Perfect circle gradient rings slicing right through the borders of the wide rectangle. `userSpaceOnUse` protects the coordinate scale from target distortion.

Test 6.6: Spread Method - Pad

Expect: Small central transition circle out to a 20% radius limit. All remaining canvas space past that threshold is cleanly clamped to solid yellow.

Test 6.7: Spread Method - Reflect

Expect: Concentric target-board ripples alternating outwards smoothly from blue to yellow, back to blue, mirroring across boundaries cleanly.

Test 6.8: Spread Method - Repeat

Expect: Concentric geometric rings showing abrupt, razor-sharp sawtooth transitions. At each 20% interval barrier, color resets immediately from yellow back to blue.

Test 6.9: Radial Transparency Blends

Expect: Dark donut vignette look. The direct core and outer edges are dark charcoal grey, fading to absolute transparent visibility at the 50% ring mark to expose the green background.
